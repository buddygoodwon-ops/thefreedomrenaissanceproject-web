Wide-open land
Wide-open futureHints below

You can build a good community almost anywhere: a dream house, good neighbors, family and friends. What you can't build is freedom where the rules won't let you. So we chose the place by its rules first.

It's in America, it's not Alaska or Hawaii, and you can fly to anywhere in the lower 48 in under five hours. We're keeping the name private until enough people have registered, to protect the effort from land speculators and early opposition.

Hint 1A county of fewer than 2,000 people.
Hint 2No state income tax.
Hint 3A town, but no city government — no city taxes or ordinances.
Hint 4No zoning or building permits. Build faster, cheaper, your way.
Hint 5Homes from about $100,000 to $400,000.
Hint 6Land around $500–$1,000 an acre. Forty acres for $20,000–$40,000.
BonusA constitutional-carry state. Freedom-minded already.

Freedom over scenery

This asks something real of you: to put liberty ahead of climate, views, restaurants and stadiums. That's exactly the trade our ancestors made. We're not asking for donations. We're asking for commitment and community.

Once you register, we'll tell you the county and walk you through what it takes to become a genuine full-time resident there.
